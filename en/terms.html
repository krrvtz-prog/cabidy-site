1. The service

TapLook is an app to organize clothing purchases and your wardrobe: a wishlist (including through an iPhone shortcut you create and run yourself, the "two taps"), the history of prices you record, importing purchases from order screenshots or receipt photos, "Own it?" for outfits you see, a wardrobe with automatic classification, outfits, trips and spending.
