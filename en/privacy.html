3. What we use it for

  • Providing the service: organizing your wardrobe, generating virtual try-ons and outfit suggestions.
  • Artificial intelligence: photos and messages are sent to AI providers (section 5) only to generate the response you asked for at that moment, and the selfie only to check it before the try-on.
  • Subscription: confirming your plan with Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Security: usage limits on AI features to prevent automated abuse, and the selfie check (one adult person, body visible).
  • Fixing errors: technical failure and app launch time logs (Sentry, in the European Union).
  • Improving the app: usage analytics (PostHog, in the European Union), only with your consent, and the anonymous count before sign-in, to understand which steps work and where people give up.
  • Promotion: learning which channels bring people in ("how did you hear about us" answer, offer codes, and Apple Ads attribution).
  • Costs and quality: monitoring failures, response time, cost of AI features, and try-on ratings.
  • Proof and transparency: keeping a record of your consents and of your acceptance of the Terms.
  • Unused accounts: emailing a notice and deleting accounts unused for 24 months (section 7).

4. Legal bases (LGPD)

  • Performance of a contract: data needed for the features you use, including your answers about wardrobe type, age range, and where you shop, which tailor the Stylist and suggestions to you, your acceptance of the Terms, and the weather forecast for trip plans.
  • Consent: for tips and news emails (optional, withdrawable in Settings or via the link in the email); for the try-on selfie (including the check before the try-on) and for sending photos and text to AI providers. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile > Settings > Withdraw AI consent: from then on nothing else is sent to AI, and AI features stay blocked until you accept again. You can also delete your selfie (Profile > Settings > Delete my selfie) or delete your account.
  • Consent (usage analytics): in every country, usage analytics is only turned on if you tick the option on the "How we use AI" screen. You can turn it on or off at any time in Profile > Settings > Help improve Cabidy.
  • Legitimate interest: abuse prevention, technical diagnostics, cost monitoring, encrypted backups, Apple Ads attribution, and the optional "how did you hear about us" answer (and the handle of who referred you), used only to learn which channels bring people in, always without content. You can skip this question, and you can delete the answer in Profile > Settings > My data. You can object to these uses through the contact in section 1.
  • Legal obligation and exercise of rights: the history of your consents and acceptances (to prove what you authorized) and subscription financial records.

The count before sign-in is anonymous: it does not identify anyone.

6. Artificial intelligence

  • Before first use, the app asks for your explicit consent to send photos and text to the AI providers (Fal.ai and Anthropic). Without that consent, no photo or text is sent to them.
  • Selfie check: before the first try-on with each selfie, Anthropic checks that the photo shows a single adult person with their body visible. If the photo appears to be of a child or teenager, shows someone else, or doesn't show the body, the try-on is not made and nothing is taken from your quota. It is an automatic estimate, with no facial recognition and no age stored.
  • Anthropic: under its commercial API terms, it does not use your messages and photos to train models and deletes them within 30 days (except content flagged for violating its usage policies, which may be kept longer for safety).
  • Fal.ai: we ask Fal not to store the requests (inputs and outputs), and the generated image goes straight back to Cabidy without remaining in Fal's history. Fal's terms allow the use of de-identified data to improve its service.
  • Cabidy does not use your photos, selfie, or conversations to train AI models.
  • We do not perform facial recognition or create biometric identifiers.
  • There are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you; suggestions are about style only.
  • AI-generated images and replies are identified as such in the app and may contain inaccuracies.

AI labeling: Images created by AI are labeled in the app and carry a machine-readable AI-generated label in the file metadata.

6-A. Usage analytics

  • We only record actions from a fixed list (for example: "added an item", "did a try-on", "viewed the subscription screen", the plan and price of a purchase, the satisfaction score) and the screens visited, linked to an internal identifier, never to your email. Along with them go the wardrobe type, age range, and "What holds you back" answer from sign-up, so we can understand usage by profile.
  • We do not record the screen, do not capture what you type, and do not use your location. The country comes from the device's region setting.
  • The data goes to PostHog, in the European Union, which does not use it for advertising.
  • Analytics is off by default and is only turned on with your consent (the option on the "How we use AI" screen). You can change this at any time in Profile > Settings > Help improve Cabidy.
  • Before sign-in, nothing goes to PostHog: we only add 1 to an anonymous count per day and step (section 2).
  • There is no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

6-B. Biometric information

Your try-on selfie is used only to generate the virtual try-on image you request and to check, before the try-on, that it shows a single adult person with their body visible. It is sent to Fal.ai (try-on) and to Anthropic (check) only for those purposes and only after your AI consent (section 6). Cabidy does not create or store face templates, face geometry, or any other biometric identifier, and does not use your selfie to identify anyone. You can delete your selfie at any time in Profile > Settings > Delete my selfie, and it is deleted together with your account (encrypted photo backups are discarded within 28 days, as described in section 7).

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: for as long as your account is active. Outfit screenshots are deleted right after the try-on (within 24 hours if a failure prevents it); we keep only the 30 most recent try-ons and the 10 most recent outfit plans; failed try-ons are deleted within 7 days.
  • Unused accounts: if you don't open the app for 24 months, we email you and, if you don't come back within 30 days, we delete the account and all its data, just like a deletion you make yourself. Accounts with an active subscription are not deleted for inactivity.
  • AI feature usage records: up to 24 months (the free try-on record is kept while the account exists, because it is allowed once per account).
  • Consent and acceptance history: up to 5 years; the latest of each type is kept while the account exists, as proof of what is in force.
  • Temporary data: usage limit records, up to 2 days; data used to avoid charging the same try-on twice, up to 24 hours.
  • Usage analytics: in PostHog for up to 12 months, deleted together with your account.
  • On your iPhone: the item list kept for offline use is discarded after 7 days without a refresh and deleted when you sign out.
  • When you delete your account (Profile > Settings > Delete account), we immediately delete your photos, selfie, try-ons, conversations, plans, reports, and account data, unless we are legally required to retain them. Encrypted database backups are discarded within 90 days, and photo backups within 28 days. We keep only: the subscription event history with no link to you (for financial records), the anonymous count before sign-in, and an anonymous exit record (account age, whether you were a subscriber, number of items and try-ons, country and, if you tell us, the reason for leaving), which does not allow anyone to be identified.

Wherever you live: anyone can request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of their data by writing to the contact email above, and most of this can be done directly in the app. We will respond within the time limits set by the applicable law.

9. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, sign-in session stored encrypted on the device with the key in the iPhone Keychain, per-user access restrictions in the database (each person can only access their own data), photos in private storage with temporary links, third-party service keys kept only on the server, and usage limits against abuse. In the event of a security incident that may cause significant risk or harm, we will notify you and the ANPD within the time limits of ANPD Board Resolution No. 15/2024.

10. Minors

Cabidy is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children or teenagers. If you become aware of such a case, write to the contact above and we will remove the data.
