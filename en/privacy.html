  • Providing the service: wishes, price history, wardrobe, purchases, outfits, trips and spending.
  • AI: reading the images you choose, classifying pieces, and creating and checking each piece's studio photo.
  • Trips: finding the destination's photo and weather forecast.
  • Subscription and credits: confirming your plan and tracking your studio photo balance.
  • Security, bug fixing, costs and quality, with usage limits against abuse.
  • Improving and promoting the app: usage measurement (only with consent), anonymous counts, "how you heard about us", offers and Apple Ads.
  • Proof of your consents, and notice and deletion of unused accounts (section 7).

4. Legal bases (LGPD)

  • Performance of contract: the features you use, your sign-up answers and acceptance of the Terms.
  • Consent: sending images to AI, usage measurement and news emails. Withdraw at any time in Profile → Settings (Withdraw AI consent; Help improve TapLook): from then on nothing else is sent, and you can still fill everything in by hand.
  • Legitimate interest: security, diagnostics, costs, backups, Apple Ads, the cancellation reason and "how you heard about us", always without content. You can object through the email in section 1.
  • Legal obligation and exercise of rights: subscription financial records and consent history.

6. Artificial intelligence

  • The only image the AI creates is the studio photo of a piece, from the cut-out of the clothing alone. It doesn't "dress" you or alter photos of people, and it is marked as AI on screen and in the file.
  • Nothing goes to AI before your explicit consent in the app.
  • The finished studio photo stays in your account for up to 24 hours, in private storage, so it arrives even if you leave the app; then it is deleted, unless it became the photo of a piece you saved.
  • Anthropic doesn't use your images to train models and deletes them within 30 days (except content flagged for usage policy violations).
  • TapLook doesn't use your photos to train AI, doesn't do facial recognition and doesn't create biometric data.
  • Reading can be wrong: everything is shown for you to check before saving. There are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and content: while the account exists or until you delete them. Price history: up to 60 per wish.
  • Screenshots and photos read by AI: not stored (except imported receipts and worn-outfit photos, until you delete them). Finished studio photo: up to 24 hours, if you don't save the piece.
  • AI usage records: up to 24 months. Usage measurement: up to 12 months. Consents: up to 5 years (the latest of each type, while the account exists). Usage limits: up to 2 days.
  • Account unused for 24 months: we notify you by email and, if you don't come back within 30 days, delete everything (except with an active subscription).
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Delete account), photos, content and account data are deleted immediately, unless the law requires otherwise. Encrypted backups are gone within 90 days (photos, 28). Only the subscription history unlinked from you, the anonymous counts and an anonymous departure record remain.

8. Your rights

You can confirm and access your data (Profile → Settings → My data), correct it, delete it (the whole account or each item on its own screen), take it with you (Export my data, as JSON), withdraw consents, object to legitimate interest and know who we share it with. For any request, email krrvtz@gmail.com: we reply within 15 days, wherever you live. You can also complain to the ANPD (gov.br/anpd) or a consumer protection body.

9. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, a login session encrypted with its key in the iPhone Keychain, each person can access only their own data, photos in private storage with temporary links, third-party keys only on the server, and limits against abuse. If an incident poses a relevant risk, we will notify you and the ANPD within the deadlines of ANPD Resolution No. 15/2024.

10. Minors

TapLook is for people 18 and over. If you know of data from a minor, write to the contact above and we will remove it.
