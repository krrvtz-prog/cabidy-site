3. What we use it for

  • Providing the service: organizing your wardrobe, generating virtual try-ons and outfit suggestions.
  • Artificial intelligence: photos and messages are sent to AI providers (section 5) only to generate the response you asked for at that moment.
  • Subscription: confirming your plan with Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Security: usage limits on AI features to prevent automated abuse.
  • Fixing errors: technical failure logs (Sentry, in the European Union).
  • Improving the app: usage analytics (PostHog, in the European Union) to understand which steps work and where people give up.
  • Costs and quality: monitoring failures, response time, and cost of AI features.
  • Proof and transparency: keeping a record of your consents.

4. Legal bases (LGPD)

  • Performance of a contract: data needed for the features you use.
  • Consent: for the try-on selfie and for sending photos and text to AI providers. You can withdraw it at any time in Profile > Settings > Withdraw AI consent: from then on nothing else is sent to AI, and AI features stay blocked until you accept again. You can also delete your selfie (Profile > Settings > Delete my selfie) or delete your account.
  • Legitimate interest: abuse prevention, technical diagnostics, cost monitoring and, in Brazil and outside Europe, usage analytics to improve the app, always without content and with the option to turn it off (Profile > Settings > Help improve Cabidy).
  • Consent (Europe): for people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, usage analytics is only turned on with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

5-A. No sale or targeted advertising

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

6. Artificial intelligence

  • Before first use, the app asks for your explicit consent to send photos and text to the AI providers (Fal.ai and Anthropic). Without that consent, no photo or text is sent to them.
  • Anthropic: under its commercial API terms, it does not use your messages and photos to train models and deletes them within 30 days (except content flagged for violating its usage policies, which may be kept longer for safety).
  • Fal.ai: we ask Fal not to store the requests (inputs and outputs), and the generated image goes straight back to Cabidy without remaining in Fal's history. Fal's terms allow the use of de-identified data to improve its service.
  • Cabidy does not use your photos, selfie, or conversations to train AI models.
  • We do not perform facial recognition or create biometric identifiers.
  • There are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you; suggestions are about style only.
  • AI-generated images and replies are identified as such in the app and may contain inaccuracies.

AI labeling: Images created by AI are labeled in the app and carry a machine-readable AI-generated label in the file metadata.

6-A. Usage analytics

  • We only record actions from a fixed list (for example: "added an item", "did a try-on", "viewed the subscription screen") and the screens visited, linked to an internal identifier, never to your email.
  • We do not record the screen, do not capture what you type, and do not use your location. The country comes from the device's region setting.
  • The data goes to PostHog, in the European Union, which does not use it for advertising.
  • In Brazil and outside Europe, analytics is on by default and can be turned off in Profile > Settings > Help improve Cabidy. In Europe, it is only turned on with your consent.
  • There is no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

6-B. Biometric information

Your try-on selfie is used only to generate the virtual try-on image you request. It is sent to Fal.ai (section 5) only for that purpose and only after your AI consent (section 6). Cabidy does not create or store face templates, face geometry, or any other biometric identifier, and does not use your selfie to identify anyone. You can delete your selfie at any time in Profile > Settings > Delete my selfie, and it is deleted together with your account (encrypted photo backups are discarded within 28 days, as described in section 7).

7. How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active. Outfit screenshots are deleted right after the try-on; we keep only the 30 most recent try-ons and the 10 most recent outfit plans. When you delete your account (Profile > Settings > Delete account), we immediately delete your photos, selfie, try-ons, conversations, plans, reports, and account data, unless we are legally required to retain them. Encrypted database backups are discarded within 90 days, and photo backups within 28 days. Usage analytics data stays in PostHog for up to 12 months and is deleted together with your account. After your account is deleted, we keep only: the subscription event history with no link to you (for financial records) and an anonymous exit record (account age, whether you were a subscriber, number of items and try-ons, and country), which does not allow anyone to be identified.

8. Your rights

  • Confirm the processing and access your data
  • Correct incomplete or outdated data
  • Delete your data (directly in the app: Profile > Settings > Delete account)
  • Portability to another provider
  • Turn off usage analytics (Profile > Settings > Help improve Cabidy)
  • Withdraw AI consent (Profile > Settings > Withdraw AI consent)
  • Delete specific data: selfie (Settings), try-ons (Try-on history, tap and hold), Stylist conversation (Delete conversation), plans (Delete plan), and items (tap and hold; the item's wear log is deleted with it)
  • Know who we share your data with

To exercise any right, write to krrvtz@gmail.com. We respond within 15 days.

Wherever you live: anyone can request access to, correction of, a copy of, or deletion of their data by writing to the contact email above, and most of this can be done directly in the app. We will respond within the time limits set by the applicable law.

9. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, sign-in session stored encrypted on the device with the key in the iPhone Keychain, per-user access restrictions in the database (each person can only access their own data), photos in private storage with temporary links, third-party service keys kept only on the server, and usage limits against abuse. In the event of a security incident that may cause significant risk or harm, we will notify you and the ANPD within the time limits of ANPD Board Resolution No. 15/2024.

10. Minors

Cabidy is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children or teenagers. If you become aware of such a case, write to the contact above and we will remove the data.

11. Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app and published on this page, with the update date at the top, before they take effect. Each acceptance is recorded with the version of this policy in force at that time.
