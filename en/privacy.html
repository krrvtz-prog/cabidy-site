3. Why we use it

  • Providing the service: wishlist, price history, wardrobe, purchases, outfits, trips and spending.
  • Artificial intelligence: images you choose are sent to Anthropic (section 5) only to be read at that moment, and photos of pieces for automatic classification. On Pro, the cutout of each piece separated from a photo goes to Fal.ai, only to create that piece's studio photo.
  • Subscription: confirming your plan with Apple and RevenueCat.
  • Security: usage limits on AI reads against automated abuse.
  • Fixing errors: technical failure logs and launch time (Sentry, in the European Union).
  • Improving the app: usage measurement (PostHog, in the European Union), only with your consent, and anonymous counts.
  • Marketing: knowing which channels bring people ("how did you hear" answer, offer codes and Apple Ads attribution).
  • Cost and quality: tracking failures, response time and cost of reads.
  • Proof and transparency: keeping the history of your consents and Terms acceptance.
  • Inactive accounts: notifying by email and deleting accounts unused for 24 months (section 7).

4. Legal bases (LGPD)

  • Performance of contract: data needed for the features you use (wishes, price history, wardrobe, purchases, outfits, trips), the sign-up answers that tailor the app's text and the Terms acceptance.
  • Consent: for sending images to AI (reading screenshots, orders, receipts and outfits, and classifying pieces) and for news emails. You can withdraw at any time in Profile → Settings → Withdraw AI consent: from then on nothing is sent to AI, and you can still fill in wishes and purchases by hand.
  • Consent (usage measurement): in every country, measurement is only on if you check the option on the "How we use AI" screen. Change it anytime in Profile → Settings → Help improve TapLook.
  • Legitimate interest: abuse prevention, technical diagnostics, cost tracking, encrypted backups, Apple Ads attribution, the optional cancellation reason and the optional "how did you hear about us" answer, always without content. You can object via the contact in section 1.
  • Legal obligation and exercise of rights: consent and acceptance history and subscription financial records.

6. Artificial intelligence

  • TapLook's AI reads the images you choose. The only image it creates is a piece's studio photo (Pro), from the cutout of the garment alone: it doesn't "dress" you, doesn't alter photos of people and marks the created photo as AI.
  • Before first use, the app asks for your explicit consent to send images to Anthropic and Fal.ai. Without it, nothing is sent; you can fill in wishes and purchases by hand.
  • The screenshot or photo travels in the request itself and is not stored on our server. We keep only the result (name, store, price, category, color) you confirm and the cut-out of a piece you save.
  • Fal.ai: on every call we ask it not to store the request or the result; the created photo goes straight back to the app and isn't on our server until you save the piece. It only receives the piece's cutout, never faces or the rest of the photo.
  • Anthropic: under its commercial API terms, it doesn't use your images to train models and deletes them within 30 days (except content flagged for usage-policy violations, which may be kept longer for safety).
  • TapLook doesn't use your photos to train AI models, doesn't do facial recognition and doesn't create biometric identifiers.
  • Reading can make mistakes (a price or a store). Everything is shown pre-filled for you to check and correct before saving.
  • There are no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6-A. Usage measurement

  • We record only actions from a fixed list and screens visited, linked to an internal identifier, never your email. The wardrobe type, age range and "What gets in your way" answer go along.
  • We don't record the screen, capture what you type, record the prices of your wishes or use your location.
  • Data goes to PostHog, in the European Union, which doesn't use it for advertising.
  • Measurement is off by default and only turned on with your consent. Before sign-in, we only add 1 to an anonymous daily count per step.
  • There is no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and content (wishes, price history, wardrobe, outfits, trips): while your account is active or until you delete them. Deleting a wish deletes its price history.
  • Screenshots and photos read by AI: not stored on our server (only the cut-out you save).
  • Price history: up to 60 prices per wish; beyond that, the oldest is removed, except the first one.
  • Inactive accounts: if you don't open the app for 24 months, we email you and, if you don't come back within 30 days, we delete the account and all its data. Accounts with an active subscription are not deleted for inactivity.
  • Cancellation reason: while the account exists.
  • AI usage logs: up to 24 months.
  • Consent and acceptance history: up to 5 years; the latest of each type stays while the account exists.
  • Temporary usage-limit data: up to 2 days.
  • Usage measurement: in PostHog for up to 12 months, deleted with the account.
  • When you delete your account (Profile → Settings → Delete account), we immediately delete photos, wishes, price history, purchases, outfits, trips, old-feature data and account data, unless the law requires otherwise. Encrypted database backups are discarded within 90 days and photo backups within 28 days. We keep only the subscription event history with no link to you, anonymous counts and an anonymous exit record (account age, whether you subscribed, number of pieces, country and, if you tell us, the reason).

8. Your rights

  • Confirm processing and access your data (Profile → Settings → My data)
  • Correct data (sign-up answers in My data; name, store, price, category and color on each wish or piece)
  • Delete your data (Profile → Settings → Delete account) or specific data (wishes: "Remove from list"; pieces, outfits and trips: on their own screen)
  • Portability (Profile → Settings → Export my data creates a JSON file, including price history)
  • Turn off usage measurement and withdraw AI consent (Profile → Settings)
  • Object to legitimate-interest uses (section 4) and know who we share your data with

To exercise any right, email krrvtz@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days. You can also petition Brazil's ANPD (gov.br/anpd) or a consumer protection body. Wherever you live, anyone can ask for access, correction, a copy or deletion of their data at the same email.

9. Security

Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, sign-in session stored encrypted on the device with the key in the iPhone Keychain, per-user access control in the database (each person only accesses their own data), photos in private storage with temporary links, price history written only by the server, third-party keys kept only on the server and usage limits against abuse. If an incident may cause relevant risk or harm, we will notify you and the ANPD within the deadlines of ANPD Resolution No. 15/2024.

10. Minors

TapLook is intended for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from children or teenagers. If you know of a case, write to the contact above and we will remove the data.
