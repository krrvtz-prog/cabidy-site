One gesture, four ways to help

1

A piece in a store

Becomes a wish with photo, store and price. If it's already on your list, today's price goes into its history.

2

An order or receipt

The pieces go into your wardrobe with price and date. A store-app screenshot or a photo of an in-store receipt.

3

Someone's outfit

“Own it?”: TapLook shows which pieces of the outfit you already have and what's missing.

4

Before you buy

A similar-piece alert, the 7-day rule and how much you saved by letting things go.

Black Friday or fake deal?

Save pieces now. On Black Friday, double-tap them again: TapLook compares with the prices you saw and flags when the “sale” was cheaper before. The history is yours, built from your taps.

How it works

  1. Install the “Save to TapLook” shortcut (1 minute, the app shows you).
  2. In any app, tap the back of your iPhone twice.
  3. Check what TapLook read and save it.

Weekly, monthly or yearly subscription, with 3 free days on the yearly plan. Includes 100 screenshot reads a month, target prices and 15 studio photos of your pieces.

Your privacy

TapLook's AI reads screenshots and only creates studio photos of your pieces (always marked as AI). Screenshots aren't kept, only the cut-out of the piece, in your account. Nothing goes to AI without your consent, no photo trains AI, and you can delete everything right in the app.
